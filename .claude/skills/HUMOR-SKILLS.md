## How to use them (Peter, 2026-10-13): restraint first

These skills are for writing the game's humorous prompts and messages
(event texts, toasts, NPC lines, shift choices, payslips). Peter's rule: do
not over-generalise and do not overdo it.

- Most text should be plain and clear. Buttons, prices, costs, hints,
  errors and anything the player has to act on are written straight, no jokes.
- Humour goes where a moment earns it: a random event, a customer's line, a
  quirk, a promotion. Roughly one light touch per screen, not one per line.
- One joke per message, at the end. Never explain it. If it isn't clearly
  funny, write it plain instead.
- Slang is seasoning: zero or one term per message, and only terms that fit
  the character saying it. Most NPCs shouldn't sound like the same person.
- Vary it. Don't reuse the same bit, structure or punchline across messages.
- Never at the player's expense for being poor, and never punching down.
- These skills don't change how Claude writes code, commit messages, docs or
  explanations to Peter.
